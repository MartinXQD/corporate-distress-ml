# Execution clarifications and corrections

The registered `V2_PROTOCOL.yaml` and its SHA256 remain unchanged. These notes distinguish operational changes from the frozen data/selection roles. All changes below precede the V2 confirmation/historical batch.

1. **ROS migration correction.** The first V2 migration of the V1 selected neural recipe used natural rather than resampled row counts for epoch-equivalent refitting. That logical trial is retained as `INVALIDATED_STEP_CONTRACT`. A new corrected trial completed all three screening years before selection. This was a V2 migration defect, not evidence of a defect in original V1.
2. **Compact masks.** The mechanism bridge used its retained source snapshot. Before main HPO, constant/duplicate binary masks were removed using training roles only; continuous raw duplicates remained in RAW18. Contract checks were rerun. Bridge and main-search implementations are explicitly identified separately.
3. **Cost-based dispatch.** The first full residual trial required about 19 monotonic minutes. Boosting families received extra turns, and uncovered mandatory families received priority. Completed folds were reused after recorded process interruptions. A wrongly inferred RF timeout was retracted: its completed fits took about 163 and 270 monotonic seconds, despite a much larger UTC gap. Warm-start forest batches preserve the seeded tree sequence, checked against direct fitting; prediction accumulation differs only within floating-point tolerance.
4. **Resource-limit scope.** The registered budget field is named `per_fold_seconds: 720`; the initial implementation applied 720 seconds to each component fit (inner stopping and full-window refit), rather than their combined fold time. Some complete fold totals can therefore exceed the nominal 720 seconds. This is an operational deviation, not a claim of exact resource-contract compliance. Actual component/fold times remain recorded; the original eight-hour UTC cap was preserved and expired without a final-model claim. It does not change labels, validation populations, stopping metrics, scoring years or registered optimizer horizons.
5. **Promotion feasibility.** Full five-year/three-seed stability, five final seeds, strong same-cutoff baselines and company stress are forecast before promotion. An unaffordable screening candidate may be omitted instead of keeping a single favorable seed. The omitted candidate and its screening score remain visible in the resource-feasibility record; omission is not evidence that its architecture is worse. Mandatory baseline references remain eligible, and the strongest same-protocol development baseline sets the ROC/Top5 guardrails.
6. **Bounded extra optimization.** At most three local rounds use development evidence. If the incumbent input is S1, matched-input boosting variants can compete with it; S1 is neither forced nor excluded. Feasible reserve may complete the original CatBoost/XGBoost targets in their existing persistent studies, still before shortlist/2014 and within the registered 5.9-hour search maximum. No historical-label adaptation is allowed.
7. **Duplicate controls and uncertainty.** A no-embedding numerical MLP can be functionally identical to the plain MLP. Such trials remain recorded but are deduplicated for effective-recipe counts and stability promotion. One set of 2,000 paired company draws is reused across all four references. Its weighted AP/ROC and paired intervals were checked against sklearn and preserved V1 bootstrap fixtures, including ties and zero weights.
8. **Preservation verification scope.** Readable protected originals are compared to the initial manifest. A system-denied private original cannot be asserted hash-identical; exact exceptions remain in the private audit, with only counts public. The earlier ignored V1 release-audit cache was briefly regenerated during setup and restored; original scientific data, models, predictions, reports and source files are checked separately. No private originals enter the public branch.

9. **Explicitly authorized continuation.** After the original window expired, the owner explicitly instructed continuing to completion. A separate eight-hour local continuation was registered before any resumed fitting: 2026-10-17 18:06:30 UTC through 2026-10-18 02:06:30 UTC. See `configs/continuation_window_02.json`. The original registration, clock, partial reports, trial databases and phase markers were preserved. This is additional authorized compute, not a retroactive extension or a claim that all work fit the first eight hours. Completed development folds are reused; there was still no V2 confirmation or historical scoring at continuation registration. Restarts do not reset either window. All final reports must disclose both windows and the actual fit calls/time.
10. **Continuation reserve.** After at least two completed recipes in each high-priority family, dispatch prioritizes 32 completed trials each for CatBoost/XGBoost before returning to the broader rotation. HPO can stop earlier if the best complete tree/neural pair needs the remaining registered-seed training reserve. Optional shortlisted candidates must fit both stability and potential final-fit costs, rather than spending the final reserve on extra stability candidates. This allocation uses development costs/scores only; it never changes the statistical protocol or substitutes a favorable seed. Actual shortfalls remain explicit.

P1 roles, P2 development years, registered seeds, the single frozen 2014 confirmation, final fit cutoff and previously inspected historical exposure retain their stated meanings. Search targets are goals: actual completion and shortfalls are reported, without counting folds, seeds or TabM heads as new methods.
